Privacy

Dad's Things is a private app our family uses to catalog and sell our father's belongings. It's only open to people on our family list.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We use them to check that you're on the family list and to record who added each item.

We also store the photos and item details family members add, such as descriptions, estimated values, and listing text.

Where it's stored

Item details, and the names and emails of family members, are kept in a Google Sheet shared within the family. Photos are stored in Google Cloud Storage. Photos can be viewed by anyone who has a photo's link, and the links are long random addresses that aren't listed anywhere public. Item photos and descriptions may be sent to an AI service to help identify and price items.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or use it for anything other than running this app.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it.

Questions or removal

To ask a question or have your information removed, email dcplatt@gmail.com.
